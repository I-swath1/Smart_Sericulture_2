UNDERSTANDING SILKWORMS

The Beautiful Silkworm Life Cycle

From a tiny egg to a silk-producing moth, discover the fascinating stages of silkworm development.

🥚
STAGE 01

Egg Stage

The life cycle begins when the female moth lays tiny eggs.

🐛
STAGE 02

Young Larva

The silkworm hatches and starts feeding on tender mulberry leaves.

🌿
STAGE 03

Mature Larva

The larva grows through five instars, with four moulting periods.

🧶
STAGE 04

Cocoon & Pupa

The mature silkworm spins a protective silk cocoon and transforms into a pupa.

🦋
STAGE 05

Adult Moth

An adult moth emerges from the cocoon and the life cycle begins again.

Did you know? The silkworm (Bombyx mori) is a domesticated insect raised for the production of natural silk.
